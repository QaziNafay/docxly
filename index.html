Docxly

Bold the headings in your Word Docx file, automatically.

Choose a .docx file. Docxly bolds the headings and subheadings in the body and leaves front matter, body text, and lists alone.

  • Files are processed by the API hosted at PythonAnywhere
  • No files are stored
  • Every other part of the file is preserved untouched
