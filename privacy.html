Privacy policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Docxly bolds the headings in Word documents. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What we process

When you use the Docxly web tool or the Docxly Word add-in, the content of the document you submit is sent to the Docxly server so it can bold the headings. Nothing else is collected.

How it is stored

Documents are processed in memory and returned to you. They are not stored, saved, shared, or used for any other purpose. The server does not keep a copy of your document.

Server logs

Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as request times and IP addresses, for operation and security. These logs are not used to identify you and are not linked to document content.

Third parties

The service is hosted on PythonAnywhere and uses HTTPS for all traffic. We do not sell or share your data with third parties. We do not use cookies to track you and we do not run advertising.

Accounts

Docxly does not require an account, and we do not collect names, email addresses, or passwords.

Children

Docxly is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will appear on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the contact link on the Docxly page.
